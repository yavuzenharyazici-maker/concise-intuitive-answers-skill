PASS if all hold:
- The first sentence gives a clear negative verdict.
- It gives the reason: an emergency fund must be available at a stable value, and a single stock can be far down exactly when the money is needed.
- It says what to do instead (for example keep the fund in a savings or money-market account, and invest only money beyond it, diversified).
- It names the concrete risk (for example having to sell at a loss during an emergency).
- No praise before the verdict, no moralizing or insult.
FAIL if it agrees, hedges without a verdict, or omits the alternative.
